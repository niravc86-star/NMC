GOOGLE SHEETS LOCAL SETUP

Place a FRESH active Google service-account JSON key named:
google-service-account.json

Supported locations:
1. Project root: google-service-account.json
2. App_Data/google-service-account.json
3. Published app folder / bin output: google-service-account.json

The old key for crm-sheet-access@profitnxcrm.iam.gserviceaccount.com returned invalid_grant/account not found and must NOT be reused.

After creating the fresh service account, share the target Google Sheet with its client_email as Editor.
For Render, use GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON instead of committing the JSON file.
